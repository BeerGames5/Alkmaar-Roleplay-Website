Cloud and security
Hosted for you, private to you.

      
The browser panel runs on our cloud and talks to your server over an outgoing connection, so it works on every host.

    

    

      
Private panel key
On first start your server creates a random 256-bit key. Only the server that holds it can answer for your panel link.

      
Your own logins
The link alone only shows a login page. Every request is checked by your server against its own staff accounts.

      
Locked down
A strict Content Security Policy, login limits per IP and logins kept per panel link.

      
No ports, no hosting
Your server opens the connection itself over HTTPS on port 443. Nothing to forward or host.

      
Copy detection
Two servers using the same key, for example from a copied database, are flagged in the console and panel.

      
Always up to date
The panel files come from the cloud, so improvements reach you without reinstalling.

FAQ
Frequently asked questions
Can't find your answer? Ask us on Discord.

    

      
Which frameworks does it support?
ESX, QBCore and Qbox. It needs ox_lib and oxmysql, which most servers already run. Inventories are supported through a bridge you can edit yourself.

      
Do I need to host anything or open ports?
No. Your server connects out to our cloud over HTTPS on port 443 and you get a private panel link. It also works on hosts that block traffic to the game port.

      
How do I get it after buying?
You log in with your Cfx.re account before paying. After payment the resource is added to your account in Keymaster. Add it to your server, set your licence and type panellink in the console.

      
Can I use it on more than one server?
One licence runs on one FiveM server at a time. Moving to another host or reinstalling is free: contact us and we release the binding.

      
Can my staff use it on their phone?
Yes. The browser panel works in any modern browser, so staff can check tickets, logs and players from anywhere.

      
Can I change how it looks?
Choose from Black, Blue, Red and Light themes, any accent colour, density, corner style and text size, and set the window size and opacity in-game.

      
What about refunds?
Purchases are final once the licence has been issued and used. If it does not work on a supported setup and we cannot fix it within 30 days, contact us and we will refund you. See the licence terms.
