Tebex has emailed your receipt. Here is how to get the panel running.

1

Download it from Keymaster

Open Keymaster → Granted assets with the Cfx.re account you used at checkout.

2

Add it to your server

Put arp-adminpanel in your resources and add ensure arp-adminpanel after ox_lib and oxmysql.

3

Set your licence

Put your licence key or Tebex transaction ID in server.cfg: set arp_panel_license "tbx-…"

4

Open your panel

Restart, type panellink in the console and create your first browser login.
